# Lessons from past jobs

Rules that apply to **every** recording. Added after the user approves a correction.
One line each, general, no timestamps. Video-specific choices go in that job's `brief.md`.

## Takes
- Every recording: use the complete take, not the first attempt.

## Cuts
- Keep the full word at every join — never clip the first or last sound.

## Visuals
- Every video: keep sources large enough to read.
