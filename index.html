Last Brick

Your old sets are still in there.

Got a box of mixed LEGO®? Last Brick helps you turn it back into sets. It's free, and your first set needs no sign-up.

  1. Add a set. Type the set number, search by name, or scan a piece.
  2. Tick off what you've found. Go through the parts list as you sort your bricks.
  3. See exactly what's missing. Last Brick lists every piece you still need, with pictures.

You can also snap a photo of a minifigure to find out who it is and which sets it came from.
