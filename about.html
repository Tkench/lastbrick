About

Hi, I'm Tracey. I built Last Brick.

I've loved LEGO® since I was very young. These days my hobby is buying bulk LEGO, the big boxes of mixed bricks you find second-hand, and finding the sets hiding inside.

  1. 1Buy a box of mixed LEGO
  2. 2Find the sets hiding in it and rebuild them
  3. 3Photograph them, take them apart and sell them on
  4. 4Use the money to buy the next box

The slow part was always working out which pieces were still missing from each set. So I built Last Brick to do it for me, and made it free so anyone with a box of LEGO can use it too.
